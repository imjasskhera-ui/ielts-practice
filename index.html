About Teen IELTS

Teen IELTS is a free online IELTS practice platform created to help students prepare for their IELTS journey.


The goal is simple: provide easy-to-use practice tools, clear questions, useful feedback and a comfortable place to practise regularly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Teen IELTS.

Is Teen IELTS free?
Yes. Teen IELTS is designed as a free online IELTS practice website.
Can I practise Reading online?
Yes. The Reading section can contain full practice tests with passages, questions, timers and results.
Can I practise Academic and General Training Writing?
Yes. The Writing section can provide separate Academic and General Training practice.
Does Teen IELTS provide an official IELTS score?
No. Teen IELTS practice results are for preparation only. Official IELTS results are provided by authorised IELTS test organisations.
Can I practise on my phone?
Yes. The website is designed to work on desktop, tablet and mobile screens.
