Passage 1 — Learning a New Language

Learning a new language can provide many benefits. It can improve communication, help people understand different cultures, and create new opportunities for education and employment.

People who speak several languages can communicate with a wider range of people. This can be particularly useful when travelling or working with people from different countries.

Language and culture are closely connected. Studying another language can introduce learners to new traditions, customs, ideas and ways of thinking.

Regular practice is essential. Reading, writing, listening and speaking can help learners remember vocabulary and become more confident. Technology can also provide opportunities to practise outside the classroom.

🗣️ IELTS Speaking

3 Parts

Part 1 — Introduction and Interview

Answer these questions naturally.

1. What do you enjoy doing in your free time?

2. Tell me about your school.

3. What is your favourite subject?

4. Do you prefer studying alone or with friends?

5. What kind of music do you like?

Part 2 — Individual Long Turn

You have one minute to prepare and should speak for one to two minutes.

Describe a skill you would like to learn.

You should say:

  • what the skill is
  • why you want to learn it
  • how you would learn it
  • and explain how it could help you

Part 3 — Discussion

Discuss these questions:

1. Why are practical skills important for young people?

2. Should schools teach more technology?

3. How might education change in the future?

4. What makes a good teacher?
