📖 Reading

Read each passage and answer Questions 1–40.

Passage 1 — Learning With Technology

Technology has become an important part of modern education. Students can use computers and mobile devices to access information, watch educational videos and complete interactive activities. These resources can make learning more flexible because students can study at different times and in different places.

However, technology is not a replacement for concentration and regular practice. Notifications, games and social media can distract students from their work. Successful learners therefore need to manage their time and decide when technology should be used for study.

Teachers also remain important. They can explain difficult ideas, give feedback and help students develop confidence.

Passage 2 — Healthy School Life

A healthy school environment can support students' physical and mental wellbeing. Regular physical activity can improve energy and concentration, while healthy food provides nutrients needed by the body.

Schools can encourage healthy habits by providing sports activities, walking programmes and information about balanced meals. Students can also benefit from learning about sleep because insufficient sleep can make concentration more difficult.

Healthy habits are not created by schools alone. Families and students also have an important role in making everyday choices.

Passage 3 — Future Skills

The world of work is changing quickly as technology develops. Many workers now use digital tools and communicate with people in different locations.

Employers increasingly value communication, teamwork, creativity, problem solving and adaptability. Workers may need to continue learning throughout their careers because technology can change the tasks they perform.

Young people can prepare for the future by developing both technical knowledge and personal skills. Education can provide opportunities to practise these abilities in realistic situations.
