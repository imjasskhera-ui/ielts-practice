Passage: The Benefits of Learning a New Language

Learning a new language can provide many benefits. It can improve communication, help people understand different cultures, and create new opportunities for education and employment. Regular practice is important because language skills improve gradually over time.

Questions

1. What can learning a new language improve?

A. Communication

B. Weather

C. Transportation

D. Cooking

2. What is important for improving language skills?

A. Sleeping

B. Regular practice

C. Travelling

D. Watching television

Answers

1. A — Communication

2. B — Regular practice
