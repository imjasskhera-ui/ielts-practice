About Teen IELTS

Teen IELTS is a free practice platform designed to help students develop the English skills they need for IELTS. The platform provides practice materials for Reading, Listening, Writing and Speaking, along with English-learning resources.


The goal is simple: practise regularly, understand your mistakes and improve step by step.
