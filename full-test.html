Passage 1: Learning in the Digital Age

Digital technology has changed the way students learn. Online resources allow learners to access information quickly and study at times that are convenient for them. Educational videos, interactive exercises and digital libraries can provide additional support outside the classroom.

However, technology does not automatically guarantee successful learning. Students still need concentration, organization and regular practice. Too many distractions can make it difficult to focus on important information.

Teachers continue to play an important role. They can explain difficult ideas, provide feedback and encourage students when learning becomes challenging.

Questions 1–5

1. What has digital technology changed?

2. What can students access quickly?

3. What can make concentration difficult?

4. What can teachers provide?

5. What does successful learning require?

Passage 2: Healthy School Communities

Schools can influence the health and wellbeing of young people. Regular physical activity, nutritious food and sufficient sleep can help students maintain energy and concentration during the school day.

Many schools provide opportunities for physical activity through sports teams, walking programmes and outdoor activities. These programmes can also help students develop teamwork and confidence.

Healthy eating is another important part of school life. Schools may provide information about balanced meals and encourage students to choose nutritious foods.

Questions 16–20

16. What can regular physical activity help students maintain?

17. Which activity is mentioned?

18. What can physical activities develop?

19. What may schools provide information about?

20. What is the passage mainly about?

Passage 3: The Future of Work

The world of work is changing as technology develops. Many jobs now require workers to use digital tools and communicate with people in different locations.

Employers increasingly value skills such as communication, problem solving, teamwork and adaptability. Workers may need to continue learning throughout their careers because new technologies can change the tasks they perform.

Education can help young people prepare for this changing environment. However, technical knowledge is not the only important factor. Creativity, communication and the ability to work with others remain valuable.
