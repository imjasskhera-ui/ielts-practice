📖 IELTS Reading

3 passages • 40 questions • 60 minutes

PASSAGE 1

Learning a New Language

Learning a new language can provide many benefits. It can improve communication, help people understand different cultures, and create new opportunities for education and employment.

People who speak several languages can communicate with a wider range of people. This can be particularly useful when travelling or working with people from different countries.

Learning a language can also help people understand traditions, customs, ideas and different ways of thinking. This can encourage respect and understanding between people.

Regular practice is essential. Reading, writing, listening and speaking regularly can help learners remember vocabulary and become more confident.

Technology can also support language learning. Educational websites, mobile applications, videos and online conversations provide opportunities to practise outside the classroom.

PASSAGE 2

Technology and Education

Technology has changed the way students learn. In the past, students depended mainly on books, teachers and classroom activities. Today, learners can use computers, tablets and mobile phones to access educational materials.

Online learning allows students to study at different times and places. Educational videos, websites and applications can provide additional practice outside school.

Technology can also make lessons more interactive. Students can watch videos, complete online exercises and communicate with other learners.

However, technology has disadvantages. Students may become distracted by games, social media and other online activities. Learners therefore need to manage their time carefully.

PASSAGE 3

Healthy Habits for Students

Healthy habits can play an important role in a student's daily life. Eating balanced meals, getting enough sleep and taking regular exercise can help young people maintain energy and concentration.

Sleep is particularly important because the brain needs time to recover and process information. Students who regularly get enough sleep may find it easier to concentrate during lessons.

Exercise can also benefit young people. Physical activity can improve fitness and provide a break from studying. Even a short walk can help students relax.

Developing healthy habits requires consistency. Small changes made regularly can become part of a student's normal routine.

🗣️ IELTS Speaking

Part 1 — Introduction

Answer naturally and give complete answers.

  1. Where do you live?
  2. What do you like about your school?
  3. What do you normally do after school?
  4. What do you enjoy doing in your free time?
  5. Do you enjoy learning English?
  6. What kind of music do you like?
  7. Do you prefer studying alone or with friends?
  8. What is your favourite subject?

Part 2 — Long Turn

Describe a skill you would like to learn.

You should say:

  • what the skill is
  • why you want to learn it
  • how you would learn it
  • and explain why it would be useful

Speak for 1–2 minutes.
