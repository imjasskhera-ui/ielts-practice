Question-reading time: You have 20 seconds to read the questions before the recording begins.

⏳ Press Start Part 1

🎙️ Listening Script

Good morning. My name is Sarah, and I am calling about student accommodation. I am starting a course at the college next month, and I would like to ask about the rooms that are available.

The accommodation office has several types of rooms. The most popular option is a single room in a shared apartment. Each apartment has a shared kitchen and living area. Students also have access to a laundry room on the ground floor.

The rent for a single room is 185 dollars per week. This includes electricity, heating and internet access. Students need to bring their own towels, but bedding is provided.

The accommodation is about fifteen minutes from the college by bus. There is also a supermarket nearby, which is convenient for students who want to prepare their own meals.

💬 IELTS Idioms & Useful Expressions

1. A piece of cake

Meaning: Something that is very easy.

Example: The grammar exercise was a piece of cake.

2. Once in a blue moon

Meaning: Very rarely.

Example: I eat fast food once in a blue moon.

3. Break the ice

Meaning: Start a friendly conversation and make people feel comfortable.

Example: The teacher told a joke to break the ice.

4. Hit the books

Meaning: Start studying seriously.

Example: I need to hit the books before my IELTS exam.

5. In the long run

Meaning: Over a long period of time.

Example: Learning another language is useful in the long run.

6. Broaden your horizons

Meaning: Increase your knowledge and experience.

Example: Travelling can broaden your horizons.

⭐ Better IELTS Expressions

Instead of: very important

Use: highly significant / extremely important

Instead of: very good

Use: highly beneficial / extremely effective

Instead of: a lot of

Use: a considerable amount of / a wide range of

Instead of: I think

Use: In my view / From my perspective
